How it works

From their story to your doorstep.

You write what you remember. We do the making, check it twice, and keep you posted at every stage.

  1. I

    Tell us their story

    A short intake form: favorite memories, proudest moments, what they want next. More detail makes a better book.

  2. II

    We build it

    Our team creates each piece from your answers, then a person reviews every one before it goes to print.

  3. III

    We print it

    Books go to our print partner. Figurines are 3D printed from the finished model. Everything is quality-checked before it ships.

  4. IV

    It arrives

    Your keepsakes ship to your door, with an email at each stage so you always know where things stand.

Questions

Before you order.

When do I pay?

Nothing is due today. This is a pre-order: you send us your graduate's details and reserve a spot. Payment is collected when your order is ready to ship.

How personal is it, really?

Very. Every piece is built from your intake form: their actual favorite memory, their real challenge, their specific goal. We review every one before print to make sure it couldn't have been written for anyone else.

Which grades is this for?

Pre-K through college. The storybook's reading level adjusts to the grade, so a kindergarten book reads differently than a 12th grade one. The comic and figurine work for any age.

How long does it take?

We work in batches. After your batch closes, allow 2–3 weeks for production and shipping. You'll get an email at every stage: Received, In Progress, Printing, and Shipped.

Can I order just one?

Yes. Order one keepsake, two, or all three. The intake form covers everything in one pass, whatever you choose.

What if something's wrong with my order?

Every order is reviewed before print, so a wrong detail or a field that didn't carry over gets caught before it ships. Reply to any of your confirmation emails and we'll fix it.
